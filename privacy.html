Privacy Policy for Rokad Hisab

Effective Date: 5 November 2026 (first published 17 July 2026)

Rokad Hisab ("the App") by Patel Studios is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App stores, uses, and protects your information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App lets you enter and store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and gender (optional profile details)
  • Financial records entered by you
  • Expense and income data
  • Borrow and lend (udhar) records
  • Travel expenses
  • Categories created by you
  • Reminder information
  • Backup files created by you

How We Use Your Information

The information you enter is used only to:

  • Provide the app's features
  • Save and organize your financial records
  • Restore backups created by you
  • Display reports and analytics inside the app

Your data is stored on your device. We do not run a server and the developer does not receive your data.

Optional Google Sign-In

No account registration or login is required to use the App. You may choose to sign in with Google to use Google Drive backup. If you do, the App receives your Google account name, email address and profile photo. These are stored on your device only, to show your account in the App and to connect Google Drive backup. They are not sent to the developer. When your Google profile photo is shown, the App loads it from Google's servers.

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the App asks for one permission: access to its own private app data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). With this permission the App can only see files that Rokad Hisab itself created. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

  • Backups are stored in your own Google Drive. Patel Studios cannot access your Drive or your backups.
  • Standard backup: the backup is stored in your Drive's private app folder and is protected by your Google account.
  • Passphrase protected backup: the backup is encrypted on your device with a key derived from a passphrase that you choose, before it is uploaded. We cannot reset or recover this passphrase. If you forget it, those backups cannot be opened.
  • The App keeps a limited number of recent backups and removes older ones.
  • App lock settings (PIN and biometric) are not included in backups.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Local Backups

You can also create a backup file on your device. This file is saved on your device or in a location you choose, and you decide whether to share it.

Internet Access

The App uses the internet only for Google sign-in, Google Drive backup and restore, loading your Google profile photo, and checking for app updates through Google Play. Without these, the App works fully offline.

App Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for Google sign-in and Drive backup.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Receive boot completed: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

Backup and restore from files use Android's built-in file access system, which lets you choose files without giving the App full access to your device's storage.

Data Collection and Sharing

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party.

Deleting Your Data

  • To stop Drive backup, open Backup & Restore in the App and choose Disconnect. This removes your Google account details from the App. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • You can delete individual Drive backups from the Backup & Restore screen.
  • You can also remove the App's access in your Google Account settings under third-party access, and delete the App's hidden data from Google Drive settings under Manage apps.
  • Uninstalling the App removes the data stored on your device.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Rokad Hisab is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated Effective Date.
