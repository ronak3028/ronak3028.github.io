Privacy Policy for Rokad Hisab

Effective Date: 14 November 2026 (first published 17 July 2026)

Rokad Hisab ("the App") by Patel Studios is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App stores, uses, and protects your information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App lets you enter and store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and gender (optional profile details)
  • Financial records entered by you
  • Expense and income data
  • Borrow and lend (udhar) records
  • Travel expenses
  • Categories created by you
  • Reminder information
  • Backup files created by you

How We Use Your Information

The information you enter is used only to:

  • Provide the app's features
  • Save and organize your financial records
  • Restore backups created by you
  • Display reports and analytics inside the app

Your financial records are stored on your device. We do not run our own server and the developer does not receive your financial records. If you create an account with Google sign-in, your account details are handled as described below.

Optional Account with Google Sign-In

No account registration or login is required to use the App. You may choose to sign up with Google to use Google Drive backup. The App uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to create and manage your account.

If you sign up, the following information is collected and stored in the App's Firebase project:

  • Your Google account name
  • Your Google account email address
  • A user ID created by Firebase for your account

When you sign in, Firebase also receives technical information automatically, such as your IP address, user agent details and the app identifier. This is described in Firebase privacy information.

We use this information only to create your account, sign you in, connect Google Drive backup, and handle your account deletion requests. Your name and email are also saved on your device to show your profile in the App. We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone other than Google as the provider of these services.

Your account details are kept until you delete your account. You can log out of the App at any time. Logging out does not delete your hisab records on your device, and you can continue to use the App without Google.

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the App asks for one permission: access to its own private app data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). With this permission the App can only see files that Rokad Hisab itself created. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

  • Backups are stored in your own Google Drive. Patel Studios cannot access your Drive or your backups.
  • Every Drive backup is encrypted on your device, before it is uploaded, with a key derived from a passphrase that you choose. We cannot reset or recover this passphrase. If you forget it, those backups cannot be opened.
  • Each installation of the App keeps one backup file in your Drive app folder. A new backup replaces the previous one from the same installation.
  • You can turn on automatic backup and choose how often it runs. To run it, the App saves your backup passphrase in encrypted storage on your device only. Turning off automatic backup, or deleting your account, removes it.
  • App lock settings (PIN and biometric) are not included in backups.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Local Backups

You can also create a backup file on your device. This file is saved on your device or in a location you choose, and you decide whether to share it.

Internet Access

The App uses the internet only for Google sign-in and account creation, Google Drive backup and restore, and checking for app updates through Google Play. Without these, the App works fully offline.

App Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for Google sign-in and Drive backup.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Receive boot completed: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

Backup and restore from files use Android's built-in file access system, which lets you choose files without giving the App full access to your device's storage.

Data Collection and Sharing

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party. Google processes your sign-in and your Drive backups as the provider of those services, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Deleting Your Account and Data

  • In the App: open More, then My Profile, then Delete account. This deletes your account and all Rokad Hisab backups in your Google Drive, and removes your backup settings from the App.
  • By email: if you cannot open the App, you can request deletion at rokadhisab@gmail.com. We delete your account within 30 days. See the account and data deletion page for details, including how to remove Drive backups yourself.
  • Your hisab records stay on your device when you delete your account. To erase them, clear the App's storage in your phone settings or uninstall the App.
  • You can also remove the App's access in your Google Account settings under third-party access, and delete the App's hidden data from Google Drive settings under Manage apps.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Rokad Hisab is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
