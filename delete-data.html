Delete Your Rokad Hisab Account and Data

Rokad Hisab by Patel Studios. Last updated: 14 November 2026

You can use Rokad Hisab without an account. If you chose to sign in with Google, the App created a Rokad Hisab account for you. This page explains how to delete that account and the data linked to it.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your Rokad Hisab account: the sign-in record kept by Firebase Authentication for the App, which holds your Google account name, email address and a user ID.
  • Your Google Drive backups: every Rokad Hisab backup stored in the private app folder of your Google Drive. This is done when you delete your account inside the App.
  • Backup settings on your phone: the automatic backup schedule and the saved backup passphrase.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Rokad Hisab and go to More, then My Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account, then tap Delete to confirm.
  3. Choose the same Google account you signed in with and allow Google Drive access when asked. This lets the App remove its backups from your Drive.
  4. Wait for the confirmation message. Your account and your Drive backups are now deleted.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Deleted Drive backups cannot be restored.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer open the App, send an email to rokadhisab@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Rokad Hisab account. Please write from the same Google email address you used to sign up, so we can match it to your account.

We will delete your Rokad Hisab account within 30 days of your request and confirm by email.

We cannot see or open your Google Drive, so we cannot delete your Drive backups for you. To remove them yourself:

  • Open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Rokad Hisab and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove the App's access to your Google account, go to your Google Account settings, open Security, then Third-party apps and services, select Rokad Hisab and remove access.

Data on Your Phone

Your hisab records, such as expenses, udhar entries, trips and reminders, are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Deleting your account does not erase them, so you can keep using the App offline. To erase them as well, clear the App's storage in your phone's settings under Apps, then Rokad Hisab, then Storage, or uninstall the App.

What We Keep

After your account is deleted, we do not keep your name, email address or user ID. Google may keep limited technical logs for a short time, as described in Firebase privacy information.
